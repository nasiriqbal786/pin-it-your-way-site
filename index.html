Pin Your Way

A better way to save and organize your ideas on Pinterest

Work in Progress

Pin Your Way is a browser extension designed to make saving and organizing your pins faster, simpler, and more intuitive. We’re building it with direct integration into the official Pinterest API so that your content is saved securely and reliably.

The extension is currently under development. Our focus is on solving common frustrations with existing tools — making it easier to select boards, manage multiple accounts, and pin content seamlessly without interruptions.

We believe creators and curators deserve a smooth, reliable experience. That’s what we’re here to build.
