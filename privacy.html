Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 25, 2025

Introduction

Pin it Your Way ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting it through this Privacy Policy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use the Pin it Your Way browser extension and related services.

Information We Collect

We collect and process the following types of information to provide and improve our services:

  • Personal Information: When you link your Pinterest account via OAuth, we receive your Pinterest user ID and basic profile information to enable pinning features.
  • Content Information: Data about the pins, boards, and images you save or interact with using the extension.
  • Usage Data: Non-identifiable technical data such as browser type, extension version, and usage patterns to optimize performance.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Facilitate the Pinterest pinning process through the extension.
  • Provide smart categorization and bulk pinning functionality.
  • Improve and personalize your experience.
  • Communicate with you about updates or support.
  • Comply with legal obligations and protect against abuse.

Pinterest API and OAuth

We use Pinterest's official OAuth system for secure authentication. We never access or store your Pinterest password. OAuth tokens are stored securely solely to enable the extension to interact with your Pinterest account.

Our use of the Pinterest API complies with Pinterest's Developer Terms and applicable data privacy laws, including GDPR and CCPA.

Cookies and Tracking

Our extension does not place cookies or use tracking technologies. However, Pinterest may place cookies on your browser in connection with OAuth and API interactions. For more details, please refer to Pinterest's own Privacy Policy.

Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We may share your data with trusted service providers under confidentiality agreements to operate and maintain the extension. We may also disclose your information to comply with legal requests or to protect rights and property.

Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information. You may revoke the extension's access to your Pinterest account anytime via your Pinterest account settings. For privacy-specific requests, please contact us.

Data Retention

We retain your information only as long as necessary to provide our services or as required by law.

Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information from unauthorized access or disclosure.

Children's Privacy

Our extension is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the new policy on our website. Your continued use of the extension after such changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
